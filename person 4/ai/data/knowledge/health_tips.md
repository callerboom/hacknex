# Health Tips & Wellness Guide
> **Disclaimer:** This is general wellness information only. It is NOT professional medical advice. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for personal medical decisions.

## Hydration
- Adults should drink at least 8 glasses (2 litres) of water per day.
- Signs of dehydration: dark urine, dry mouth, dizziness, fatigue.
- Increase water intake during exercise, hot weather, or illness.
- Oral Rehydration Solution (ORS) for moderate dehydration: mix 1 litre clean water + 6 teaspoons sugar + 0.5 teaspoon salt.

## Fever Management
- Normal body temperature: 36.1°C to 37.2°C (97°F to 99°F).
- Mild fever (37.5°C–38.5°C): rest, fluids, paracetamol if needed.
- High fever (above 39.5°C / 103°F): seek immediate medical attention.
- Cool the patient with a damp cloth. Do NOT use ice baths.
- Never give aspirin to children under 16.

## Common Cold & Flu
- Rest and sleep are the most effective treatments.
- Drink warm liquids (broth, herbal tea, warm water with honey and lemon).
- Use saline nasal spray to relieve congestion.
- Paracetamol or ibuprofen for pain and fever. Follow dosage instructions.
- See a doctor if symptoms last more than 10 days or worsen suddenly.

## Headache Relief
- Tension headaches: rest in a quiet dark room, hydrate, apply cold or warm compress.
- Migraine: rest, avoid bright lights and screens, ibuprofen or prescribed medication.
- Red flags requiring urgent care: sudden severe headache, headache with stiff neck, vision changes, or headache after head injury.

## Minor Cuts & Wounds
- Clean hands before treating.
- Rinse wound under clean running water for at least 5 minutes.
- Apply gentle pressure with a clean cloth to stop bleeding.
- Apply antiseptic cream and cover with a sterile bandage.
- Change dressing daily. Watch for signs of infection: redness spreading, pus, warmth, fever.
- Seek medical care for deep wounds, wounds that won't stop bleeding, or animal bites.

## Breathing Difficulty
- Sit upright. Loosen tight clothing.
- Breathe slowly and deeply through the nose.
- If caused by asthma, use prescribed inhaler immediately.
- Seek emergency help if lips or fingernails turn blue, breathing is very laboured, or person loses consciousness.

## Chest Pain
- Any unexplained chest pain is a medical emergency.
- Call emergency services immediately.
- Help the person sit or lie down in a comfortable position.
- If they are conscious and not allergic, aspirin 300mg may be given while waiting for help.
- Do NOT leave the person alone.

## General Wellness
- Sleep 7 to 9 hours per night for adults.
- Eat a balanced diet with vegetables, proteins, whole grains, and fruits.
- Exercise at least 150 minutes of moderate activity per week.
- Avoid smoking and excessive alcohol.
- Manage stress with breathing exercises, walking, or relaxation techniques.
