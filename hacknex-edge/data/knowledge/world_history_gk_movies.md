## 1. Major World History Eras and Pivotal Turning Points

- **Ancient Civilizations**:
  - **Mesopotamia** (Tigris & Euphrates): Invention of cuneiform writing, wheel, Code of Hammurabi (early written legal code).
  - **Ancient Egypt** (Nile River): Pyramids of Giza, hieroglyphics, pharaohs (Tutankhamun, Ramses II).
  - **Indus Valley Civilization** (Harappa and Mohenjo-daro): Advanced urban drainage systems, grid-planned brick cities, bronze metallurgy.
  - **Ancient Greece**: Athens (birthplace of democracy, philosophy: Socrates, Plato, Aristotle), Sparta, Alexander the Great's empire (356–323 BCE).
  - **Roman Republic & Empire**: Julius Caesar, Augustus (first Roman Emperor 27 BCE), Pax Romana, Roman Law, fall of the Western Roman Empire in 476 CE.
- **Medieval and Early Modern Eras**:
  - **Byzantine Empire**: Capital Constantinople (fell to Ottoman Turks under Mehmed II in 1453 CE).
  - **Islamic Golden Age**: Baghdad House of Wisdom, algebra (Al-Khwarizmi), medicine (Ibn Sina / Avicenna).
  - **Mongol Empire**: Genghis Khan (1206), largest contiguous land empire, opened the Silk Road trade.
  - **European Renaissance**: 14th–17th century resurgence of science, art, literature (Leonardo da Vinci, Michelangelo, Gutenberg printing press c. 1440).
  - **Scientific & Industrial Revolutions**: Copernicus, Galileo, Newton; James Watt's steam engine (18th century Britain), rise of factories and mechanization.
- **20th Century World Conflicts**:
  - **World War I (1914–1918)**: Triggered by assassination of Archduke Franz Ferdinand; Allied Powers (UK, France, Russia, later US) vs Central Powers (Germany, Austria-Hungary, Ottoman Empire); ended with Armistice on Nov 11, 1918 and Treaty of Versailles (1919).
  - **World War II (1939–1945)**: Began with Nazi Germany invasion of Poland (Sept 1, 1939); Allies (UK, USSR, USA, China) vs Axis (Germany, Italy, Japan); Holocaust; D-Day Normandy invasion (June 6, 1944); Atomic bombings of Hiroshima (Aug 6) and Nagasaki (Aug 9, 1945); Japan surrender on Sept 2, 1945.
  - **Cold War (1947–1991)**: Geopolitical tension between USA (NATO) and USSR (Warsaw Pact); Korean War (1950–1953), Cuban Missile Crisis (1962), Space Race (Sputnik 1957, Apollo 11 Moon landing 1969); Fall of Berlin Wall (1989), dissolution of Soviet Union (Dec 1991).

## 2. Indian History and Independence Movement

- **Ancient & Classical India**: Vedic period, Maurya Empire (Chandragupta Maurya, Emperor Ashoka and the spread of Buddhism, Edicts of Ashoka), Gupta Empire ("Golden Age" of Indian science and mathematics: Aryabhata, zero, decimal system, Kalidasa).
- **Medieval Indian Empires**: Chola Dynasty (naval power, Brihadeeswarar Temple, Rajaraja Chola, Rajendra Chola), Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara Empire (Krishnadevaraya), Mughal Empire (Babur, Akbar, Shah Jahan builder of Taj Mahal, Aurangzeb).
- **Freedom Struggle**:
  - Revolt of 1857 (Sepoy Mutiny / First War of Independence); British Crown takes direct control from East India Company.
  - Formation of Indian National Congress (1885).
  - Mahatma Gandhi: Ahimsa (non-violence), Satyagraha, Champaran (1917), Non-Cooperation Movement (1920), Dandi Salt March (1930), Quit India Movement (1942).
  - Revolutionary leaders: Bhagat Singh, Chandrashekhar Azad, Subhash Chandra Bose (Indian National Army / Azad Hind Fauj).
  - Independence: August 15, 1947; Partition of India and Pakistan; Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel integrated 565+ princely states.
  - Constitution: Adopted on November 26, 1949; came into effect on Republic Day, January 26, 1950. Dr. B.R. Ambedkar was Chairman of the Drafting Committee.

## 3. World Geography & General Knowledge

- **Earth & Oceans**:
  - 7 Continents: Asia (largest by area and population), Africa, North America, South America, Antarctica, Europe, Australia/Oceania.
  - 5 Oceans: Pacific (largest, deepest - Mariana Trench 11,034m), Atlantic, Indian, Southern, Arctic.
  - Highest Mountain: Mount Everest (Himalayas, 8,848.86 m).
  - Longest River: Nile River (~6,650 km); Largest River by water discharge: Amazon River.
  - Largest Desert: Antarctic Desert (polar); Largest Hot Desert: Sahara Desert.
- **Key International Organizations**:
  - **United Nations (UN)**: Headquarters in New York City; founded Oct 24, 1945.
  - **World Health Organization (WHO)**: Headquarters in Geneva, Switzerland.
  - **International Court of Justice (ICJ)**: Headquarters at Peace Palace, The Hague, Netherlands.
  - **International Monetary Fund (IMF) & World Bank**: Headquarters in Washington, D.C.
- **Space Milestones**:
  - First artificial satellite: Sputnik 1 (USSR, 1957).
  - First human in space: Yuri Gagarin (USSR, 1961).
  - First humans on the Moon: Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin (Apollo 11, USA, July 20, 1969).
  - Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO): Chandrayaan-1 (discovered water on moon), Mangalyaan / Mars Orbiter Mission (first Asian nation in Mars orbit on first attempt), Chandrayaan-3 (historic soft landing near lunar South Pole in August 2023).

## 4. World Cinema, Indian Cinema, and Pop Culture

- **Pioneers & Foundations of Cinema**:
  - Auguste and Louis Lumière: First public projected motion picture screening in Paris (1895).
  - Silent Era & Classical Hollywood: Charlie Chaplin (The Kid, Modern Times, City Lights), Buster Keaton, Orson Welles (Citizen Kane - groundbreaking cinematography and deep focus).
- **Master Filmmakers & Iconic Hollywood Works**:
  - **Stanley Kubrick**: 2001: A Space Odyssey, The Shining, A Clockwork Orange.
  - **Alfred Hitchcock**: "Master of Suspense" - Psycho, Rear Window, Vertigo.
  - **Steven Spielberg**: Jaws (first summer blockbuster), Raiders of the Lost Ark, Jurassic Park, Schindler's List (Best Director/Picture Oscar).
  - **Christopher Nolan**: Non-linear narrative master - Inception, Interstellar, The Dark Knight Trilogy, Memento, Oppenheimer (Oscar winner).
  - **Martin Scorsese**: Taxi Driver, Raging Bull, Goodfellas, The Wolf of Wall Street.
  - **James Cameron**: Terminator, Aliens, Titanic (11 Oscars), Avatar (pioneered 3D and performance capture).
  - **Quentin Tarantino**: Pulp Fiction (Palme d'Or), Kill Bill, Django Unchained, Inglourious Basterds.
- **Indian Cinema Heritage**:
  - **Dadasaheb Phalke**: Father of Indian Cinema; directed first Indian full-length feature film *Raja Harishchandra* (1913).
  - **Satyajit Ray**: Global auteur; *The Apu Trilogy* (Pather Panchali, Aparajito, Apur Sansar); awarded Academy Honorary Award (Oscar) in 1992.
  - **Tamil Cinema (Kollywood)**:
    - Pioneers: M.G. Ramachandran (MGR), Sivaji Ganesan (expressive dramatic acting).
    - Superstars: Rajinikanth (pop culture icon, Baashha, Sivaji, Enthiran), Kamal Haasan (method acting, Nayakan, Indian, Hey Ram, Vikram).
    - Directors: Mani Ratnam (Nayakan ranked in TIME 100 films, Thalapathi, Roja, Bombay, Iruvar, Ponniyin Selvan), K. Balachander, Shankar, Vetrimaaran (Asuran, Aadukalam), Lokesh Kanagaraj (Kaithi, Vikram, Leo).
    - Music Maestros: Ilaiyaraaja ("Isaignani", revolutionized Indian film orchestrations combining folk with Western counterpoint), A.R. Rahman ("Mozart of Madras", Roja, Bombay, Dil Se, Oscar winner for *Slumdog Millionaire* - Best Original Score and Song "Jai Ho").
  - **Hindi (Bollywood) & Telugu (Tollywood)**:
    - Amitabh Bachchan, Shah Rukh Khan ("King of Bollywood").
    - S.S. Rajamouli: Baahubali franchise, RRR (won Golden Globe & Academy Award for Best Original Song "Naatu Naatu").
- **Cinema Terms & Technology**:
  - Cinematography: Lighting, framing, rule of thirds, depth of field, color grading.
  - Aspect Ratios: 1.33:1 (Academy 4:3), 1.85:1 (Standard Widescreen), 2.39:1 (Anamorphic scope), 1.43:1 (IMAX).
  - CGI & VFX: Computer-Generated Imagery; Motion Capture; Practical Effects.
