## 1. Classical Mechanics & Dynamics

- **Newton's Three Laws of Motion**:
  1. **First Law (Inertia)**: An object remains at rest or continues in uniform motion in a straight line unless acted upon by a net external force.
  2. **Second Law (Force & Acceleration)**: The net force on an object equals the rate of change of its momentum: $\vec{F}_{\text{net}} = m\vec{a}$.
  3. **Third Law (Action-Reaction)**: Whenever one object exerts a force on another, the second object exerts an equal and opposite force on the first: $\vec{F}_{AB} = -\vec{F}_{BA}$.
- **Kinematic Equations (Constant Acceleration)**:
  - $v = u + at$
  - $s = ut + \frac{1}{2}at^2$
  - $v^2 = u^2 + 2as$
  - $s = \frac{(u + v)}{2} t$
  (where $u$ = initial velocity, $v$ = final velocity, $a$ = acceleration, $t$ = time, $s$ = displacement).
- **Work, Energy and Power**:
  - Work: $W = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{d} = F d \cos \theta$ (Joules, J).
  - Kinetic Energy: $KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$.
  - Gravitational Potential Energy: $PE = mgh$.
  - Work-Energy Theorem: $W_{\text{net}} = \Delta KE$.
  - Power: $P = \frac{W}{t} = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}$ (Watts, W).
- **Universal Gravitation**:
  - Newton's Law of Gravitation: $F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$, where $G = 6.674 \times 10^{-11} \, \text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$.
  - Acceleration due to gravity at Earth's surface: $g = \frac{GM}{R^2} \approx 9.81 \, \text{m/s}^2$.

## 2. Thermodynamics, Fluid Dynamics & Waves

- **Laws of Thermodynamics**:
  - **Zeroth Law**: If systems A and B are each in thermal equilibrium with C, then A and B are in thermal equilibrium with each other (basis of temperature).
  - **First Law (Conservation of Energy)**: $\Delta U = Q - W$, where $\Delta U$ is change in internal energy, $Q$ is heat added, and $W$ is work done by the system.
  - **Second Law (Entropy)**: The total entropy of an isolated system never decreases over time: $\Delta S \geq 0$. Heat cannot spontaneously flow from a colder body to a hotter body.
  - **Third Law**: As temperature approaches absolute zero ($0 \, \text{K} = -273.15^\circ\text{C}$), the entropy of a pure crystalline substance approaches a minimum constant (zero).
- **Heat Transfer**:
  - Specific heat: $Q = mc\Delta T$.
  - Latent heat: $Q = mL$ (for phase transitions).
- **Fluid Mechanics**:
  - Continuity Equation: $A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2$.
  - Bernoulli's Principle: $P + \frac{1}{2}\rho v^2 + \rho gh = \text{constant}$.
- **Wave Motion & Sound**:
  - Wave equation: $v = f \lambda$ (speed = frequency $\times$ wavelength).
  - Speed of sound in air at $20^\circ\text{C}$: $\approx 343 \, \text{m/s}$.
  - Doppler Effect: $f' = f \left(\frac{v \pm v_o}{v \mp v_s}\right)$.

## 3. Electromagnetism & Modern Physics

- **Electrostatics**:
  - Coulomb's Law: $F = k_e \frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2}$, where $k_e = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \approx 8.99 \times 10^9 \, \text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$.
  - Electric Field: $\vec{E} = \frac{\vec{F}}{q}$.
- **Electric Circuits**:
  - Ohm's Law: $V = IR$ ($V$ in Volts, $I$ in Amperes, $R$ in Ohms $\Omega$).
  - Electrical Power: $P = VI = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}$.
  - Resistors in Series: $R_{\text{total}} = R_1 + R_2 + \dots$
  - Resistors in Parallel: $\frac{1}{R_{\text{total}}} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \dots$
  - Kirchhoff's Current Law (KCL): Sum of currents entering a junction equals sum of currents leaving ($\sum I = 0$).
  - Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL): Sum of electrical potential differences around any closed network loop equals zero ($\sum V = 0$).
- **Magnetism**:
  - Lorentz Force: $\vec{F} = q(\vec{E} + \vec{v} \times \vec{B})$.
  - Faraday's Law of Induction: $\mathcal{E} = -\frac{d\Phi_B}{dt}$.
- **Quantum & Relativistic Physics**:
  - Planck-Einstein Relation: $E = hf = \frac{hc}{\lambda}$, where $h = 6.626 \times 10^{-34} \, \text{J}\cdot\text{s}$, $c = 3.0 \times 10^8 \, \text{m/s}$.
  - De Broglie Wavelength: $\lambda = \frac{h}{p} = \frac{h}{mv}$.
  - Mass-Energy Equivalence: $E = mc^2$.

## 4. Chemistry: Periodic Table & Atomic Structure

- **Atomic Structure**:
  - Protons (+1 charge, atomic number $Z$).
  - Neutrons (neutral, mass number $A = Z + N$).
  - Electrons (-1 charge, orbital shells).
  - Electron Configuration Rules:
    - Aufbau Principle: Electrons fill lowest energy subshells first ($1s, 2s, 2p, 3s, 3p, 4s, 3d \dots$).
    - Pauli Exclusion Principle: No two electrons can have identical four quantum numbers ($n, l, m_l, m_s$).
    - Hund's Rule: Every orbital in a subshell is singly occupied before being doubly occupied.
- **Periodic Trends**:
  - **Atomic Radius**: Decreases across a period (left to right) due to increasing effective nuclear charge; increases down a group.
  - **Ionization Energy**: Increases across a period; decreases down a group.
  - **Electronegativity**: Increases across a period; decreases down a group (Fluorine is highest at 4.0 on Pauling scale).
- **Key Elements**:
  - Hydrogen (H, Z=1), Helium (He, Z=2), Carbon (C, Z=6), Nitrogen (N, Z=7), Oxygen (O, Z=8), Sodium (Na, Z=11), Chlorine (Cl, Z=17), Iron (Fe, Z=26), Gold (Au, Z=79).

## 5. Chemical Bonding, Stoichiometry & Reactions

- **Bonding Types**:
  - **Ionic Bond**: Electrostatic attraction between cations and anions (transfer of electrons, e.g., $\text{NaCl}$).
  - **Covalent Bond**: Sharing of electron pairs between nonmetals (e.g., $\text{H}_2\text{O}, \text{CH}_4$). Polar if electronegativity difference is between 0.4 and 1.7.
  - **Metallic Bond**: "Sea of delocalized electrons" surrounding metal cations.
  - **Intermolecular Forces**: Hydrogen bonding (H bonded to N, O, or F), dipole-dipole, London dispersion forces.
- **The Mole Concept & Gas Laws**:
  - Avogadro's Number: $N_A = 6.022 \times 10^{23} \, \text{mol}^{-1}$.
  - Moles: $n = \frac{\text{mass}}{\text{molar mass}}$.
  - Ideal Gas Law: $PV = nRT$, where $R = 8.314 \, \text{J}/(\text{mol}\cdot\text{K}) = 0.0821 \, \text{L}\cdot\text{atm}/(\text{mol}\cdot\text{K})$.
  - Standard Temperature and Pressure (STP): $0^\circ\text{C}$ (273.15 K) and $1 \, \text{atm}$; $1 \text{ mole of ideal gas} = 22.4 \, \text{L}$.
- **Acids, Bases & pH**:
  - Arrhenius: Acids produce $\text{H}^+$; bases produce $\text{OH}^-$.
  - Brønsted-Lowry: Acids donate protons; bases accept protons.
  - Lewis: Acids accept electron pairs; bases donate electron pairs.
  - $\text{pH} = -\log_{10}[\text{H}^+]$, $\text{pOH} = -\log_{10}[\text{OH}^-]$, $\text{pH} + \text{pOH} = 14$ at $25^\circ\text{C}$.

## 6. Organic Chemistry Fundamentals

- **Hydrocarbons**:
  - Alkanes (Single bonds, saturated): General formula $C_n H_{2n+2}$ (Methane $\text{CH}_4$, Ethane $\text{C}_2\text{H}_6$, Propane $\text{C}_3\text{H}_8$).
  - Alkenes (Double bond, unsaturated): $C_n H_{2n}$ (Ethene $\text{C}_2\text{H}_4$).
  - Alkynes (Triple bond): $C_n H_{2n-2}$ (Ethyne / Acetylene $\text{C}_2\text{H}_2$).
  - Aromatic Hydrocarbons: Benzene ring ($\text{C}_6\text{H}_6$).
- **Functional Groups**:
  - Alcohols: $-\text{OH}$ (e.g., Ethanol $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH}$).
  - Aldehydes: $-\text{CHO}$ (e.g., Formaldehyde $\text{HCHO}$).
  - Ketones: $R-\text{CO}-R'$ (e.g., Acetone $\text{CH}_3\text{COCH}_3$).
  - Carboxylic Acids: $-\text{COOH}$ (e.g., Acetic acid $\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}$).
  - Esters: $-\text{COOR}$ (Pleasant fruit aromas).
  - Amines: $-\text{NH}_2$.
