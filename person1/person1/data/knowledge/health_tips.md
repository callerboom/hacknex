# General Health, Wellness & Daily Care Guidelines
> **IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER**: This offline knowledge base is for informational and educational purposes only. It is **not professional medical advice**, diagnosis, or treatment. Always seek the advice of a physician or qualified healthcare provider with any questions you may have regarding a medical condition. In an emergency, contact local emergency services immediately.

---

## 1. Daily Hydration Guidelines
- **Recommended Intake**: Aim for 2.0 to 3.0 liters (8–12 glasses) of water daily for adults under moderate activity levels.
- **Signs of Dehydration**:
  - Dark yellow or amber urine
  - Dry mouth, lips, or cracked tongue
  - Fatigue, dizziness, or lightheadedness
  - Mild to moderate headache
- **Best Practices**:
  - Drink a glass of water upon waking up.
  - Hydrate before, during, and after physical exertion.
  - In hot weather or heavy sweating, replenish electrolytes (sodium, potassium, magnesium) using coconut water, oral rehydration solutions (ORS), or light broths.

## 2. Headache & Migraine Relief
- **Tension Headache**:
  - Rest in a quiet, dimly lit room.
  - Apply a cool or warm compress to forehead or base of neck for 15 minutes.
  - Gently massage temple, neck, and shoulder muscles.
  - Drink a large glass of water to rule out dehydration.
- **Migraine Management**:
  - Minimize sensory stimuli (lights, loud sounds, screen glare).
  - Stay hydrated and maintain consistent sleep schedules.
  - Avoid known dietary triggers (excess caffeine, aged cheese, artificial sweeteners).
- **When to Seek Immediate Medical Help (Red Flags)**:
  - Sudden, severe "thunderclap" headache.
  - Headache accompanied by fever, stiff neck, confusion, weakness, numbness, or difficulty speaking.
  - Headache following head trauma.

## 3. Sleep Hygiene & Quality Rest
- **Target Duration**: 7 to 9 hours of uninterrupted sleep for adults.
- **Sleep Optimization Checklist**:
  - Maintain a consistent bedtime and wake-up time, even on weekends.
  - Avoid screens (blue light) at least 45–60 minutes before bed.
  - Keep bedroom cool (18°C–20°C / 65°F–68°F), dark, and quiet.
  - Avoid heavy meals, alcohol, and caffeine within 4–6 hours of sleep.
  - Establish a relaxing wind-down routine (reading, light stretching, journaling).

## 4. Fever Management & Comfort
- **Normal Range**: 36.5°C to 37.5°C (97.7°F to 99.5°F). Low-grade fever is typically 37.8°C–38.3°C (100°F–101°F).
- **Home Comfort Measures**:
  - Rest adequately to allow immune defenses to work.
  - Stay well-hydrated with water, warm teas, and clear soups.
  - Wear lightweight, breathable clothing and use a light blanket.
  - Apply lukewarm (not cold or icy) sponge baths if feeling overheated.
- **When to Contact a Doctor**:
  - Fever exceeding 39.4°C (103°F) in adults, or lasting longer than 3 consecutive days.
  - Accompanying severe throat swelling, rash, persistent vomiting, or difficulty breathing.

## 5. Cold, Cough & Seasonal Flu Care
- **Symptom Support**:
  - **Sore Throat**: Warm salt-water gargle (1/2 tsp salt in 1 cup warm water) 3–4 times daily. Honey and warm lemon water soothe throat irritation.
  - **Congestion**: Inhale steam over a warm bowl of water or take a warm shower; use saline nasal sprays.
  - **Cough**: Stay upright while resting, use a humidifier, and drink plenty of fluids.
- **Prevention**: Wash hands frequently with soap for at least 20 seconds; avoid touching eyes, nose, and mouth.

## 6. Desk Ergonomics & Posture
- **The 20-20-20 Rule**: Every 20 minutes, look at an object 20 feet away for at least 20 seconds to reduce eye strain.
- **Chair & Screen Setup**:
  - Position top third of monitor at or slightly below eye level.
  - Keep elbows at a 90-degree angle with wrists straight while typing.
  - Keep feet flat on the floor or supported by a footrest.
  - Take standing or stretching breaks every 45–60 minutes.

## 7. Mental Wellbeing & Stress Reduction
- **Box Breathing (4-4-4-4)**:
  1. Inhale slowly through nose for 4 seconds.
  2. Hold breath for 4 seconds.
  3. Exhale smoothly through mouth for 4 seconds.
  4. Hold empty lungs for 4 seconds. Repeat 4 cycles.
- **5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Technique**:
  - Acknowledge 5 things you can see, 4 things you can feel, 3 things you can hear, 2 things you can smell, and 1 thing you can taste.
- **Daily Movement**: Even a 15-minute brisk walk improves mood, cardiovascular health, and focus.
