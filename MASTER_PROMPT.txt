1. MISSION & COMPETITION OVERVIEW
You are a senior Edge-AI and Performance Systems Engineer on a 4-person team. We are building the final, complete, working entry for hackathon problem HNX26EPS08: "On-Device Conversational Stack" (HackNEX 2026).
The system is an offline, CPU-only, ultra-low latency voice-to-voice assistant running on Windows, featuring:
1. Real-time microphone listening and Voice Activity Detection (VAD).
2. Local Streaming Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR).
3. Instant Rule-based Router + In-memory BM25 Knowledge Retrieval + Local GGUF LLM.
4. Sentence-chunked Text-to-Speech (TTS) with cached responses.
5. Strict, proven hardware resource limits (CPU cores & RAM) enforced at the OS level.
6. A live FastAPI + WebSocket telemetry dashboard showing latency waterfalls and resource gauges.

Every module must be real, fully runnable, and measured. There are NO mocks, NO stubs, NO cloud calls, and NO GPU dependencies. Live judging is conducted on our physical laptop with unseen spoken questions.
